Controller test

Plug the DualSense in with a USB cable, choose Connect controller, then press Cross (the × button). You should feel four short taps.

Everything on this page also works from the keyboard. Keys 1 to 4 play 1 to 4 taps, F plays the complex pattern, R reads the controller state, Escape stops all vibration.

With a screen reader in browse mode, those single keys go to the screen reader instead. Every action is also a button on this page: move to it with Tab and press Enter. In NVDA, NVDA+Space switches to focus mode, where the single keys reach the page. Controller buttons work in either mode.

No controller connected. Plug the controller in with a USB cable and choose Connect controller.

Taps

On the controller: Square 1 tap, Triangle 2 taps, Circle 3 taps, Cross 4 taps, Options the complex pattern. The NMR page uses 1 to 4 taps for a singlet to a quartet and the complex pattern for anything else (doublet of doublets, multiplet).




The gap range 80 to 250 ms is the range the NMR page will use for coupling constants: a wider gap for a larger J. Try a few and note which counts are easiest to feel.

Trigger resistance

Pick a pattern, then pull R2 or L2 slowly. The trigger travel is split into 10 zones; each zone resists at a level from 0 (loose) to 8 (stiffest). On the controller, L1 and R1 step through the patterns.
